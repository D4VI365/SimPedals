# 5/20/2026 8 PM - Initial research

_Time spent: 1.25h_

Ok, so to go with the custom DD wheel I'm also designing these pedals. The design itself probably won't be that complicated but now I'll only explain the working principle.
To avoid problems caused by wear and dirt the accelerator and clutch pedal will use ss49e hall effect sensors and magnets to get the position. To make it more realistic the brake pedal will use a load cell (still to decide the value) to measure the force applied to the pedal and not the position itself. To make resistance every pedal will have a compression sping with some sort of adjustable stiffness. Everything will be powered by an arduino nano as it doesn't need anything powerful to get the values and transmit them to the computer. for the design and mechanical working principle I will take inspiration from [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=44LWekyILmk) as it looks really well made and adjustable.
